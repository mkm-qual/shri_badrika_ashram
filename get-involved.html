Sri Hari Vatika

In the peaceful surroundings of Sri Badrika Ashram lies Sri Hari Vatika, a sacred garden where flowers are grown with devotion and care. Every bloom is cultivated organically with sunlight, pure water, and loving seva. These flowers are offered daily to Sri Hari Bhagwan, becoming garlands that carry the prayers of every devotee.

The Vatika reflects the ashram’s purity, devotion and sustainability. Every plant is treated as sacred, and devotees are welcome to contribute to its care and upkeep. Offerings made here help this divine garden flourish, where every flower blooms only for Bhagwan.

Sri Hari Ann Bhandar

At Sri Badrika Ashram, food is regarded as prasād — a divine gift. Offering a meal is one of the most sacred forms of seva, known in our tradition as Annadānam, the donation of food. When you sponsor a meal, you are not merely feeding seekers; you are offering nourishment to the Divine present in all beings.

Sri Hari Chikitsa Kendra

The Sri Hari Chikitsā Kendra was inaugurated by Swamiji on 30th December 2016, with the pure intention of “Jagat Kalyān” (service to all beings). At the inauguration, Swamiji lovingly remarked: “Here at Sri Hari Temple, you receive spiritual health, and at the Chikitsā Kendra, you receive physical health.”

What began as a small room with two dedicated doctors travelling daily from Solan has now grown into a center of care and compassion. The Kendra provides free treatment and medicines to Ashram residents and nearby villagers, becoming a true symbol of seva and Sri Hari’s grace.

General Corpus

If you feel inspired to support the Ashram’s mission, you may contribute to the General Corpus Fund. Your offering is directed where it is most needed, maintaining essential services, supporting devotees, funding education and health projects, and meeting unforeseen requirements.

Each contribution strengthens the Ashram’s ability to serve with care and devotion. By contributing, you become a guardian of Sanātan Dharma, helping preserve its timeless values while sustaining the spiritual and social initiatives that touch countless lives.
