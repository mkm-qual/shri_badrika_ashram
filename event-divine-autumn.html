A Journey into Grace and Stillness

Divine Autumn

Before winter descends upon the Himalayas, there comes a rare season of stillness.

The air turns crisp. The skies deepen. The mountains seem to fall silent. And somewhere within that silence, the heart begins to listen.

This autumn, Sri Badrika Ashram invites you to take a sacred pause from the world outside and gently return to the world within.

Come into the presence of Sri Hari.
Come where silence feels alive.
Come where grace is not sought, but simply felt.
