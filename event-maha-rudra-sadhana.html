The Return of Rudra · in English

Maha Rudra Sadhana

Shiva is the eternal source from which all creation flows - the silence before sound, the witness beyond time.

Last year, on this sacred land, Swamiji invoked Bhagwan Rudra, and now, once again, by His boundless grace, the Lord returns. This Maha Shivratri, following the sacred Abhishekam, the Maha Rudra Sadhana begins again. Join us as the grace of Rudra flows through every open heart.

Before you book

Important Booking Guidelines – Please Read Carefully

Review every guideline before proceeding with your booking.

  1. Devotees who participated in Maha Rudra Sadhana 2026 (Hindi) will not be eligible to participate in Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English).
  2. If you participate in Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English), you will not be permitted to attend any other Swamiji events or discourses from November 2026 to April 2027.
  3. If either of the above conditions is violated, the Ashram reserves the right to cancel the booking, and the amount paid will be non-refundable and non-transferable.
  4. The language of this Sadhana will be English.
  5. Booking is available only for devotees participating for the full 11-day duration. Short-term or partial participation will not be permitted.
  6. Children below 10 years of age will not be permitted in the Ashram for this Sadhana.
  7. Devotees with physical limitations or conditions that may prevent them from participating fully in the Sadhana are requested not to register for this event.

Personal Meetings with Swamiji

There will be no personal meetings with Swamiji during the entire duration of the Sadhana.

Optional

Yajamana Registration

Dakshina per person, per Yajna

₹5,100

Once your booking is confirmed, you will receive an email invitation within one week to register as a Yajamana if you wish to participate physically in the Yajna. Yajamana registration is completely optional.

Those who do not register as Yajamana may still participate in the Yajna by performing the rituals mentally along with Swamiji.

Yajamana seats are limited and will be allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.

The Yajamana registration email should reach you within two to three weeks of completing your booking.
