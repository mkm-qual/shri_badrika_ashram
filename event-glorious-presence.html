The Glorious Presence

There are a total of six events under the name The Glorious Presence, held at the Ashram from November 2026 to April 2027.

Each event includes three discourse dates with Swamiji, along with non-discourse dates you may stay for. Swamiji will be present only on the Discourse Date.

If you are not attending Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English), you may book a maximum of 3 of these events.

Before you book

Important Booking Guidelines – Please Read Carefully

Review every guideline before proceeding with your booking.

  1. There are a total of six events under the name The Glorious Presence. If you are not attending Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English), you may book a maximum of 3 of these events.
  2. Participants attending “The Children of Tomorrow” may attend a maximum of 2 of these events.
  3. Participants attending Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English) are not eligible to attend these events. Participants who attended Maha Rudra Sadhana 2026 (Hindi) will not be eligible to participate in Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English).
  4. Children below 10 years of age are not permitted to stay at the Ashram during these events.
  5. This event includes both discourse and non-discourse dates. A booking may be made for a minimum of 2 nights and for any additional dates available within that event. Please carefully check the schedule, particularly the dates on which Swamiji will be giving discourse, before making your booking.
  6. Participants with any physical limitations or health-related conditions are kindly requested not to register for this event.
  7. Day visits are not permitted at any time during the entire duration of the event.

Personal Meetings with Swamiji

Personal meetings with Swamiji will be held on discourse days only and will be selected through a draw system.
