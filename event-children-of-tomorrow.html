The Children of Tomorrow

After almost a decade, Swamiji has decided to conduct a retreat exclusively dedicated to young minds—the children who will shape the world of tomorrow.

The Children of Tomorrow is a three-day journey to nurture young minds, awaken their potential, and plant the seeds of wisdom and purpose.

Because the future is not something we wait for; it is something we nurture today. And every great future begins with a seed.

Before you book

Important Booking Guidelines (Please Read Carefully)

Review every guideline before proceeding with your booking.

  1. For this particular event, children below 10 years of age are allowed in the Ashram.
  2. Children must be accompanied by a parent or grandparent. No other relatives will be permitted to accompany them.
  3. Children below 7 may stay at the Ashram but cannot attend the discourse.
  4. Parents/grandparents attending this event will not be permitted to participate in Maha Rudra Sadhana 2027 (English) and may attend a maximum of two discourse events under name The Glorious Presence. The specific guidelines for both events will be mentioned in their respective descriptions once they are launched. Kindly wait for the events to be launched.
  5. Kindly note that this is a general discourse event, with children being welcomed as a special exception. Earlier, children were not permitted to attend Swamiji’s events due to the Ashram’s emphasis on silence. The event will follow the regular Ashram schedule, without separate activities specifically for children.

If any of the above conditions are not followed, the Ashram reserves the right to cancel the booking. In such cases, the amount paid will be non-refundable and non-transferable.
